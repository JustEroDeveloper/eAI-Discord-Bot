# 🔒 Security & Disclaimer

## Privacy

- Your `DISCORD_TOKEN`, `GITHUB_TOKEN` and any other secrets live **only in your local `.env`** file, which is git-ignored and must never be committed or shared.
- The bot talks to exactly three services you configure yourself: **Discord**, a **local Ollama** instance, and a **local or self-hosted SearXNG** instance. No telemetry, no third-party servers.
- The optional **mac controller** (`mac-controller/BotController.swift`) controls the bot on your own machine only.

## Security notes

- **Never commit your `.env`** — the bot token gives full control of your Discord bot.
- Use a **separate bot token** for development; reset it in the Discord Developer Portal if it leaks.
- Only give `/announce`, `/changelog`, `/pinlatest` and `/setchannels` to users you trust — they require **Manage Messages** permission, but Discord permissions should always be the real gate.
- `channels.json` contains server IDs only (no secrets), but is git-ignored anyway.
- The bot runs shell-free by default; review `search.py` and `ollama_client.py` before adding anything that executes commands.
- If you expose Ollama or SearXNG to your network, protect those endpoints yourself (see the main eAI SECURITY guidance).
